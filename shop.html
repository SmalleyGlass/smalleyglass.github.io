The Legal Bit (Returns & Refunds)

Standard shipping is UK only. International orders by request.

I am a real person, not a faceless warehouse. If you are not happy with your glass, you have 14 days from the day you receive it to change your mind and let me know.

How to cancel: Just contact me to say you’d like to return it.

Sending it back: You have another 14 days to post the item back to me. Unless the item is faulty, you are responsible for the cost of return postage. Please pack it carefully; if it arrives back in pieces, I cannot refund you.

Refunds: Once I receive the item back in one piece, I will refund the price of the item to your original payment method within 14 days.

Exceptions: Custom commissions or personalised items cannot be returned unless they are faulty.
